## Project name

Fossils and Archeology Rebirth

## Short summary

Dig up fossils, bring prehistoric animals back to life, and explore ancient ruins. A Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3.

## Description

Fossils and Archeology Rebirth brings dinosaurs and other prehistoric animals to Minecraft. Start by digging up fossils, recover DNA with an Analyzer, and use a Culture Vat to prepare eggs and embryos. Hatch them, build a home for your animals, and look after them as they grow.

There are 64 prehistoric species, from large land animals to ocean predators and flying reptiles. Animals have different diets and behaviors. Predators hunt, timid animals flee, and some herbivores defend themselves. You can tame animals, give them commands, and ride certain species.

There is plenty to find outside your enclosures, too. Explore ruins, temples and volcanic terrain, restore old artifacts, and put together skeleton displays for your own museum. The Dinopedia helps you learn about your animals and the machines you use. You can also work toward the Anu encounter and its treasury.

### What is included

- 64 prehistoric species, including land, water and flying animals.
- Fossil excavation, DNA analysis, culture and incubation.
- Animal feeding, taming, breeding, commands and riding for supported species.
- Five machines: Analyzer, Culture Vat, Sifter, Worktable and Feeder.
- Skeleton displays, pottery, restored artifacts and ancient equipment.
- Prehistoric plants, fossil and amber deposits, ruins, temples and volcanic terrain.
- Archaeologist and paleontologist villagers.

### Requirements

This version is for **Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric** and requires **Java 25** and **Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer**.

Install these Fabric libraries alongside the mod:

- Fabric API
- GeckoLib
- TerraBlender
- SmartBrainLib
- Structure Pool API

Team Reborn Energy is already included inside the mod jar.

### About Rebirth

Rebirth is maintained by **gemdYT** and builds on the original Fossils and Archeology mod. Some systems have been simplified or rebuilt for modern Minecraft, while keeping fossil excavation, animal care and archaeology at the center of the mod.

Version **0.0.1** is the first Rebirth release. The main gameplay systems have had focused testing, but animal balance, long play sessions, modpacks and dedicated-server gameplay still need more testing. If you find a problem, please report it with your mod version, steps to reproduce it and a log if you have one.
